# Animation Guidelines

## Animation Philosophy
- Animation is storytelling, not decoration.
- Every motion must be motivated by emotion or narrative.
- Silence and stillness are as important as movement.

## Timing
- Favor long, deliberate beats in the opening.
- Use brief motion only to support discovery.
- Pause often to let the audience breathe.

## Ease
- Use smooth, natural easing.
- Avoid sharp acceleration or abrupt stops.
- Motion should feel organic and grounded.

## Motion Hierarchy
- Primary: camera motion and scene pacing.
- Secondary: subtle environment movement (dust, nebula, stars).
- Tertiary: small detail motion only when it reinforces mood.

## Animation Categories
- **Director controlled**: pacing, scene transitions, camera mood.
- **Environment**: stars, dust, nebula, black hole disk.
- **Decorative**: fine grain, ambient drift.

## Director Controlled Animations
- The director defines the beat and mood, not the render layer.
- Systems should interpret high-level intent rather than hard-coded cues.
- Scene progress and transitions are orchestrated in intention space.

## What Must Never Be Animated
- No fast camera cuts.
- No flash or explicit bloom.
- No sudden appearance of objects.
- No forced UI-style movement.
