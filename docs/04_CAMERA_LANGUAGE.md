# Camera Language

## Camera Principles

- The camera is a storyteller, not a controls surface.
- Motion must feel intentional, not mechanical.
- Silence and stillness are as important as movement.

## Movement Rules

- Keep movement slow and continuous.
- Favor smooth transitions over abrupt changes.
- Avoid any feel of orbiting or game-style exploration.

## Forward Motion Philosophy

- Forward movement should suggest a vessel drifting through space.
- It should feel inevitable and calm, not rushed.

## Idle Motion

- Idle drift is subtle and organic.
- Small oscillation creates life without distraction.
- Stillness is part of the performance.

## Acceleration

- Use gentle ramps.
- Avoid sudden bursts.
- Acceleration should feel weighted and deliberate.

## Deceleration

- Slow the camera into frame with intention.
- Let motion settle before the next beat.

## Roll

- Minimal roll only to support composition.
- Use roll as a soft adjustment, not a stunt.

## Drift

- Drift is the primary behavior.
- Base movement on low-frequency curves.
- Keep drift amplitude small and constant.

## Future Portal Movement

- Portal-style movement is not part of the opening.
- If used later, it should preserve the calm, cinematic tone.
